Four moving parts. All on-chain.

Each module is its own contract with a single job, so you can read exactly what it does.

01

Community Claim

400M $DTA waiting for the community. Connect your wallet, we check your Merkle proof, you claim. One transaction. The list is locked the moment claims open.

02

Holder Rewards

A 110M pool paid out in epochs, 2% of what is left each time. It is a finite program, not perpetual yield, and every epoch is published for anyone to verify.

03

LP Vault

Back the pool. Deposit $DTA/ETH LP tokens, get dLP shares for your slice of the vault, and withdraw whenever you like. No owner can touch what is inside.

04

Buyback & Burn

ETH goes in, $DTA is bought on a Base DEX and sent straight to the burn address. Less supply, in public, with every transaction on the dashboard.

Built so you do not have to trust us.

Fixed supply

Everything is minted once at deployment. Nobody, including the team, can print more.

Multisig from block one

Privileged contracts are owned by a multisig from their first block. The deployer key keeps nothing.

Locked claim list

The claim list freezes when claims open, and the deadline can only move later, never sooner.

Capped rewards

Epochs have a minimum gap and a hard per-epoch cap, so the pool cannot be drained in a rush.

Hardened vault

An ERC-4626 vault with inflation-attack protection and no admin keys.

Guarded buybacks

Slippage limits, size caps and a cooldown on every buyback. Bought tokens can only be burned.
